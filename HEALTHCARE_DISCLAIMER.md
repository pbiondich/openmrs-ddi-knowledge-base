# Healthcare disclaimer

This repository contains drug-drug interaction data and tooling intended to
support clinical decision *support*, not to replace it.

- This data is not medical advice and is not a substitute for the judgment of
  a qualified clinician. Prescribing decisions remain the responsibility of the
  treating provider.
- The interaction records are reformatted from DDInter 2.0, an academic
  database. Coverage is incomplete: the absence of a drug pair here does not
  mean the combination is safe. Where a pair is missing, treat it as a
  knowledge gap and consult another authoritative source, never as a
  "no interaction found" result.
- Severity classifications and mechanism descriptions come from DDInter and
  have not been independently re-validated for any specific patient, dose,
  route, or population.
